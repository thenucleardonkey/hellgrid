# Hellgrid

A demon-wave shooter in a 3D grid maze, made with three.js in a single HTML file.

**Play:** https://thenucleardonkey.github.io/hellgrid/

WASD or arrows to move, Space or F to fire, R to reload, right mouse button or E to scope. Click the view for mouse look; Esc opens the menu, where you choose Solo or Co-op and the difficulty, and can open Settings to rebind keys. Pick a perk before every wave and survive eight waves.

Co-op on the website has proximity voice chat: turn on your microphone in Settings › Voice chat, then hold V to talk. Teammates hear you louder the closer you are, from the direction you're in, and muffled through walls.
